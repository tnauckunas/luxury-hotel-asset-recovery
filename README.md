# Luxury Hotel Asset Recovery
Operational Recovery & Inventory-Control Case Study | ~300 Luxury-Hotel Bedding Assets

# Executive Summary

During a large-scale luxury hospitality project, approximately 300 high-value bedding assets required operational recovery following handling, storage and traceability issues.

I helped lead the recovery process by introducing a structured workflow covering asset identification, condition triage, cleaning, rewrapping, tagging and location control. The objective was to restore visibility over the affected inventory, improve retrieval accuracy and reduce the risk of unnecessary asset replacement.

The recovery programme helped bring the affected assets back under structured control while mitigating potential replacement exposure estimated at approximately £350,000.
